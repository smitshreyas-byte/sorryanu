Chapter 1

Dear Anu...

I don't know where to begin...

Maybe with a simple "I'm sorry."

Not because those two words can erase your pain... but because they are the truest words in my heart today.

I know this website won't magically fix everything.

I know your tears were real.

I know your silence after that moment carried more pain than any argument ever could.

And knowing that I was the reason is something that hurts me every single day.

I wish I had protected your heart the same way you always protected mine.

Today I'm not asking you to forget.

I'm only asking you to listen to my heart... one last time.
